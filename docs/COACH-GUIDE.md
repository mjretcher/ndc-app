# Coach quick-start guide

Sign in at the app's address with the email and password your admin gave you.
On a phone, tap the browser menu → **Add to Home Screen** to get an app icon.

## The one-minute tour

The bottom bar (phone) / sidebar (desktop) has five areas:

- **Today** — today's practices, anything that needs your attention
- **Calendar** — the schedule; tap any practice
- **Divers / Families** — everyone's info, memberships, and billing plans
- **Billing** — charges, monthly review, and invoices (admin issues them)
- **More** — memberships tracker, registrations, reports, settings (admin)

## New family joins

1. Send them the public link: **`/register`**. One form covers the whole
   family — multiple divers included — and works fine on a phone.
2. Their submission appears on **Today** and under **Registrations**.
3. Open it, read everything (medical notes are highlighted), pick each diver's
   **group** and **billing plan**, and tap **Approve & create family**. They
   get a welcome email automatically. You can also ask for more info or reject.
4. If they still need AAU / USA Diving memberships, the **Memberships** page
   shows the gap — one tap emails the family the step-by-step guide.

## Running a practice (the important one)

1. On **Today**, tap the practice → **Take attendance**.
2. Tap a status on each diver: **Here / Out / Excused / Trial**. Tap again to
   un-mark. Every tap saves immediately — a pulsing dot means "saving".
3. A kid from another group showed up? Use **Add a diver from another group**.
4. Making a practice free for someone? After marking Here, tap
   *"Billing normally — tap to waive"* and give a reason.
5. Done? Tap **Mark remaining N absent**.

Billing happens by itself: per-practice divers get the right rate for the day
(Sundays cost more automatically), monthly-plan divers don't get per-practice
charges, trials are free by default, and canceled practices never bill. If you
fix a mistake later, the charge fixes itself too — no duplicates, ever.

## Schedule changes

- **New practice** on the Calendar → recurring series (put Sundays in their own
  series so Sunday pricing applies) or a one-off/clinic.
- Editing or canceling asks: *just this one, this & future, or the whole
  series* — and whether to **email the affected families**. No email goes out
  unless you check that box.

## Month-end money (owner/admin)

1. **Billing** → open the month. Monthly plan charges generate automatically.
2. The review page flags problems first: divers with no plan, attendance that
   couldn't be priced ("NEEDS REVIEW"), practices missing attendance.
3. Skim the charges by family; **Waive** anything that shouldn't bill.
4. **Build invoices** → check each family's total → **Issue** (one or all).
   Issuing assigns the invoice number, emails the family, and locks history.
5. Record checks/cash on the invoice as they come in. Made a mistake on an
   issued invoice? **Void it** (with a reason) — its charges return for
   re-invoicing and any used credit comes back. Numbers are never reused.

Discounts, scholarships, credits, and manual charges all live on the
**family page**, and everything money-related is written to the audit log.

## Good to know

- Medical details are visible to coaches only — never in emails or exports.
- **Reports** exports anything to CSV for Excel/Sheets.
- Old spreadsheets? Settings → Import/Export has a CSV template + previewed,
  duplicate-checked import.
